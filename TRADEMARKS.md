[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The name and the logo are not: the licence gives you no right to
use them (section 6 of the licence). This policy says how you may.

We want you to build on this code, fork it and talk about it. We also want people to know that
something called Ilkal comes from us, so they are not misled.

## What you may do

- **Say what your product uses or works with.** "Built with Ilkal", "works with Ilkal" and
  "uses the Ilkal SDK" are fine, in plain text, as long as they are true.
- **Redistribute the SDK unchanged.** You may ship `@ilkal/sdk` as we publish it, under its own
  name, inside your product.
- **Refer to us truthfully.** You may use the name "Ilkal" to refer to us and our products in
  articles, tutorials, reviews, comparisons and talks.

## What you may not do

- **Name your product or fork Ilkal.** A product, fork, package, domain, app or company may not
  be called Ilkal, or a name that could be confused with it ("Ilkal Pro", "Ilkal-Community",
  "ilkal-next").
- **Use our logo or wordmark.** This includes the mark, the wordmark and anything that looks
  like them, unless we have agreed to it in writing.
- **Suggest that we endorse you.** Don't imply that we make, sponsor, certify or support your
  product when we don't.

## Forks

If you change the code and distribute it, give it another name and another logo, and remove ours.
You may say that it is "based on the Ilkal SDK" or "forked from Ilkal".

## Questions

If you're not sure whether a use is fine, or you'd like to do something this policy doesn't
allow, write to [support@ilkal.app](mailto:support@ilkal.app). We may update this policy; the
version in this repository is the current one.
